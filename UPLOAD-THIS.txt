STUDIO BROWNE — what to upload to GitHub
========================================
Everything in THIS folder. 66 files. Nothing else on your
computer needs to go up.

   6 web pages      index, work, broadway, jackson, filbert, vallejo
  60 photos         inside the assets folder

Do it in two drags, not one. The uploader has silently dropped
files on you before when given too many at once.


DRAG 1 — the pages
------------------
In your repository, click "Add file" > "Upload files".

Select the six .html files in this folder and drag them in
together. Do NOT drag the assets folder in this pass.

Commit message:  Pages
Click "Commit changes".


DRAG 2 — the photos
-------------------
"Add file" > "Upload files" again.

Drag in the single folder called  assets

Wait for all 60 to finish before committing. If the count on
screen is lower than 60, cancel and drag the assets folder
again — do not commit a short upload.

Commit message:  Photos
Click "Commit changes".


CHECK IT
--------
Give it about a minute, then open:

  https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/REPO-NAME/

  - Homepage should show Jackson with the blue Victorian
  - Work page should list 6 projects, Filbert at 03
  - broadway.html and jackson.html should both open
