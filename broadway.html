Broadway

Pacific Heights, SF · 2019 · Full-Service Residential
About the Project

A full-height entry built around a single commissioned piece — a blackened steel ribbon that turns through three floors, carrying perforated brass bowls that throw light across the plaster.

The house asked for restraint everywhere else. Limestone laid in slabs, walnut cut thin and lit from within, a kitchen reduced to stone and shadow. The custom work carries the drama so the rooms can stay quiet.
