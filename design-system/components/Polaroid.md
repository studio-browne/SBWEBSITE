**Polaroid** — the studio's instant-film image frame: thick white border, deep bottom lip, soft warm shadow, optional tilt and caps caption. Used to present a single hero photograph or a team grid.

```jsx
<Polaroid image="assets/images/project-living.png" caption="Regent's Park · London" ratio="4 / 5" width="360px" />
<Polaroid image="..." tilt={-2} />
```
