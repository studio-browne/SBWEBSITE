**PullQuote** — oversized light-serif quotation with clay quote marks, for client testimonials and studio philosophy statements.

```jsx
<PullQuote cite="— A. Whitfield, Trousdale Residence">
  They understood the house before we did.
</PullQuote>
<PullQuote tone="light" align="center">A home should feel inevitable.</PullQuote>
```

Use `tone="light"` inside dark editorial sections.
