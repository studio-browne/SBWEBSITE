**Input** — a bare underline field (no box), serif value, caps label. The studio's understated form control; used on inquiry / contact forms.

```jsx
<Input label="Full name" placeholder="Jane Appleseed" />
<Input label="Email" type="email" hint="We reply within two business days." />
<Input label="Message" tone="light" />
```

Focus deepens the underline to clay. Pass `tone="light"` on dark grounds.
